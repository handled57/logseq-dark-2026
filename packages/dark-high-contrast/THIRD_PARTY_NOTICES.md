## Bible reference manifest

`resources/bible.books.json` is a reference manifest: book names, chapter counts, verse counts and verse-id offsets. It carries no verse text. It is generated by `scripts/build-bible-index.mjs` from a per-verse export of *The SBL Study Bible* (New Revised Standard Version, Updated Edition), and its chapter and verse counts, and the omission of the verses that edition rejects as textually doubtful, follow that edition.

The verse text itself is not part of this repository or of any release archive. The text index the Passage command reads is built locally from an edition the reader already holds, and the generator writes it to a git-ignored path that no packaging step includes.
